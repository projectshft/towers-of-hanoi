Towers of Hanoi

A simple JavaScript console game based on the classic Towers of Hanoi puzzle.

Rules

  • Only one disc can be moved at a time
  • Only the top disc on a peg can be moved
  • A larger disc cannot be placed on top of a smaller disc

How to Play

Open the browser console and use: moveDisc(from, to);

New game, board is now:
--- 5 4 3 2 1
---
---

// Move the disc from peg 1 to peg 2
moveDisc(1, 2);
✔ That move was successful, board is now:
--- 5 4 3 2
--- 1
---

// Move disc from peg 1 to peg 3
moveDisc(1, 3);
✔ That move was successful, board is now:
--- 5 4 3
--- 1
--- 2

// Move disc from peg 1 to peg 2
moveDisc(1, 2);
✘ You cannot move a larger disc on top of a smaller one, board is still:
--- 5 4 3
--- 1
--- 2

Move all discs from the first peg to another to win the game.
